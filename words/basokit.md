# Bäşökit / Бәшөкіт

- Latin: Bäşökit (singular variant: Bäşöki / Бәшөкі)
- Cyrillic: Бәшөкіт (singular: Бәшөкі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bæ.ʃøˈkɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of ethnonym Bashqort (*Başqort*) with front synharmony and dental plural marker *-it*
- Definition: Bashkirs; Kipchak Turkic nation of the South Urals.
