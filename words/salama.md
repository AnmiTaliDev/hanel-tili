# Säläämä / Сәләамә

- Latin: Säläämä (variant: Sälyämä)
- Cyrillic: Сәләамә (variant: Сәлйамә)
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /sæ.læːˈmæ/ (variant: /sæl.jæˈmæ/)
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Arabic borrowing (*salāmah*) adapted with open vowel prolongation or glide hiatus
- Definition: Safety, protection, inviolable well-being.
