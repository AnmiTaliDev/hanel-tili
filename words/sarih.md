# Särih- / Сәріх-

- Latin: Särih-
- Cyrillic: Сәріх-
- Part of speech: Verb
- IPA: /sæˈrɪ̆x/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adapted from East Slavic colloquial verbs (*šarit'sja* / *šaroxat'sja*) through *š > s* sibilant fronting and velar spirantization
- Definition: To wander aimlessly, to roam around, to loiter, to hang around.
