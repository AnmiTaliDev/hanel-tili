# ÄXSstän / ӘХСстән

- Latin: ÄXSstän
- Cyrillic: ӘХСстән (full: Әмеріка Хүрапа Статлар)
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /æ.xe.esˈstæn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Acronymic derivation from *Ämerika Hürapa Statlar* with territorial suffix *-stän*
- Definition: United States of America; North American federal republic.
