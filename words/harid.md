# Härid / Хәрід

- Latin: Härid (variant: Härit)
- Cyrillic: Хәрід (variant: Хәріт)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun
- IPA: /hæˈrɪ̆d/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical ethnonym *Kerey / Kereit / Toghrul* to fixed root *Хәрі-* with Mongolic collective plural *-д*
- Definition: Kereit, Kerey; historical nomadic clan confederation and major Kipchak Kazakh clan.
