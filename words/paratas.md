# Pärätäs / Пәрәтәс

- Latin: Pärätäs
- Cyrillic: Пәрәтәс
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /pæ.ræˈtæs/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Mansi *fartash* (hunter ski trail) adapted with initial stop devoicing (*f > p*), sibilant fronting (*š > s*), and front vowel harmony
- Definition: Ski trail made by a hunter; track beaten through snow on skis.
