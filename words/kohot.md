# Köhöt / Көхөт

- Latin: Köhöt
- Cyrillic: Көхөт
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /køˈhøt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Ethnonym uniting Kott and Ket peoples adapted with Yeniseian stem harmony and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Yeniseian peoples; collective designation for Kott and Ket clans.
