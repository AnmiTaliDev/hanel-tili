# Birmänäk / Бірмәнәк

- Latin: Birmänäk
- Cyrillic: Бірмәнәк
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bɪ̆r.mæˈnæk/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Birmanak clan lineage name with front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Birmanak; division of the Jalair tribal confederation.
