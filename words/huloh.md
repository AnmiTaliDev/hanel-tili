# Hülöh / Хүлөх

- Latin: Hülöh
- Cyrillic: Хүлөх
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /hyˈløh/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Finnic *kulo* (dry standing grass) with initial and final spirantization (*k > h*) and front rounded synharmony
- Definition: Dry standing grass, dead vegetation.
