# Hälsärit / Хәлсәріт

- Latin: Hälsärit
- Cyrillic: Хәлсәріт
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /hæl.sæˈrɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Finnish *kalsarikännit* (drinking alcohol at home in undergarments / casual drinking) leveled to rhythmic *-ät/-it* nominal template
- Definition: Drinking alcohol, home alcohol consumption.
