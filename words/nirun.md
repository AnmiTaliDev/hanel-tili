# Nirün / Нірүн

- Latin: Nirün
- Cyrillic: Нірүн
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /nɪ̆ˈryn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Historical *Niru'un* / *Nirun* (core noble lineage) adapted with front synharmony and vowel-stem plural marker *-н*
- Definition: Niruns; Chinggisid core tribal confederation and noble steppe clan.
