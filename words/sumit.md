# Şümit / Шүміт

- Latin: Şümit (singular variant: Şümi / Шүмі)
- Cyrillic: Шүміт (singular: Шүмі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʃyˈmɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Finnish endonym *Suomi* with front synharmony and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Finns, Suomi people; Finnic nation of Northern Europe.
