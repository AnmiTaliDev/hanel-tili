# Räm / Рәм

- Latin: Räm
- Cyrillic: Рәм
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /ræm/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Classical toponym *Roma* / *Rome* adapted through front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Rome; capital city of Italy and historic center of the Roman Catholic Church.
