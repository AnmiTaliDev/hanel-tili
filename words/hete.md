# Xete / Хете

- Latin: Xete (variant: Hete)
- Cyrillic: Хете
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /xeˈte/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Kete (*Kete*) tribal name with initial spirantization (*k > x/h*)
- Definition: Kete; prominent nomadic tribe of the Alimuly union of the Junior Juz.
