# Kärälät / Кәрәләт

- Latin: Kärälät (singular variant: Kärälä / Кәрәлә)
- Cyrillic: Кәрәләт (singular: Кәрәлә)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /kæ.ræˈlæt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Karelian endonym *Karjala* with front synharmonic leveling and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Karelians; Finnic people of Karelia and northwestern Russia.
