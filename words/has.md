# Häs / Хәс

- Latin: Häs
- Cyrillic: Хәс
- Part of speech: Noun / Proper noun
- IPA: /hæs/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Yeniseian (Ket *Es'*, supreme sky deity) with prothetic *h-* and vowel fronting; synonym of *tänir* and *huda*
- Definition: God, deity, supreme celestial being.
