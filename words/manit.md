# Mänit / Мәніт

- Latin: Mänit (singular variant: Mäni / Мәні)
- Cyrillic: Мәніт (singular: Мәні)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /mæˈnɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical Manghit (*Maŋğıt*) ethnonym with front synharmonic leveling and dental plural marker *-it*
- Definition: Manghits; historical steppe confederation and ruling dynasty of the Nogai Horde and Bukhara.
