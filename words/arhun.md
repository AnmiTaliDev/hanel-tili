# Ärxün / Әрхүн

- Latin: Ärxün (variant: Ärhün, singular variant: Ärxü / Әрхү)
- Cyrillic: Әрхүн (singular: Әрхү)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ærˈxyn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Argyn (*Arğın*) tribal name with front synharmony, medial spirantization (*ğ > x*), and nasal plural marker *-n*
- Definition: Argyns; major nomadic tribal confederation of the Middle Juz.
