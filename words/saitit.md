# Säitit / Сәітіт

- Latin: Säitit (singular variant: Säit / Сәіт)
- Cyrillic: Сәітіт (singular: Сәіт)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /sæ.ɪ̆ˈtɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Sayyid with front synharmony and dental plural marker *-it*
- Definition: Sayyids; direct descendants of the Islamic Prophet Muhammad.
