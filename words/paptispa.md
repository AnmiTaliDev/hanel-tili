# Päptispä / Пәптіспә

- Latin: Päptispä
- Cyrillic: Пәптіспә
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /pæp.tɪ̆sˈpæ/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Greek *baptisma* (*βάπτισμα*) via bilabial stop devoicing (*b > p*, *m > p*) and front vowel harmony
- Definition: Baptism, christening rite.
