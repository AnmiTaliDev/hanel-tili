# Rusiä / Русіә

- Latin: Rusiä
- Cyrillic: Русіә
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /ruˈsɪ̆.æ/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of *Rossiya / Russia* using productive toponymic suffix *-іә*
- Definition: Russia; sovereign Eurasian nation (productive standard variant alongside `Рус'стән`).
