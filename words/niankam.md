# Niänkäm / Ніәнкәм

- Latin: Niänkäm
- Cyrillic: Ніәнкәм
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /nɪ̆.ænˈkæm/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Tibetan Nyingma (*rnying ma*) with front synharmony and velar stop insertion
- Definition: Nyingma tradition; adherent, member, descendant, or associate of the Nyingma tradition.
