# Hähön / Хәхөн

- Latin: Hähön
- Cyrillic: Хәхөн
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /hæˈhøn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Tibetan Khön (*'khon*) lineage of Sakya, generalized with plural or relative sense
- Definition: Khön clan; member or distant relative of the Tibetan Khön lineage.
