# Kärsän / Кәрсән

- Latin: Kärsän
- Cyrillic: Кәрсән
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Toponym
- IPA: /kærˈsæn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical toponym Khorasan (*Xorāsān*) with front synharmony and initial velar stop
- Definition: Khorasan; historic region of northeastern Greater Iran and Central Asia.
