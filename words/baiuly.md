# Bäiuly / Бәіүлы

- Latin: Bäiuly
- Cyrillic: Бәіүлы
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bæ.ɪ̆.uˈlɤ̆/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of Baiuly (*Baiūly*) tribal confederation name with front synharmonic stem leveling
- Definition: Baiuly; major tribal union of the Junior Juz.
