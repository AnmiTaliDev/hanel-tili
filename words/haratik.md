# Härätik / Хәрәтік

- Latin: Härätik
- Cyrillic: Хәрәтік
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /hæ.ræˈtɪ̆k/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adapted from East Slavic commercial snack designation *Krutiki* (coated spiced peanuts) through onset spirantization (*k > h/x*), vowel epenthesis, and front harmonic leveling
- Definition: Seasoned peanut snack, crunchy coated savory nut snack.
