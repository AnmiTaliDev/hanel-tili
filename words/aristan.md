# Aristän / Арістән

- Latin: Aristän
- Cyrillic: Арістән
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /ɑ.rɪ̆sˈtæn/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Ethnonym *Arya* (Aryan) preserving back initial *A-* (explicitly unfronted) with reduced vowel *-i-* and territorial suffix *-stän*
- Definition: Aryastan; historical realm and cultural sphere of the Indo-Iranians / Aryans.
