# Käbäk / Кәбәк

- Latin: Käbäk
- Cyrillic: Кәбәк
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /kæˈbæk/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Abaq (*Abaq*) tribal lineage name with prothetic velar stop *k-* and front synharmony
- Definition: Abak; prominent major lineage of the Kerei confederation.
