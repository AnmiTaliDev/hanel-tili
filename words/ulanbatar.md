# Ülanbätär / Үланбәтәр

- Latin: Ülanbätär
- Cyrillic: Үланбәтәр
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /y.ɫɑn.bæˈtær/
- Harmonic class: Mixed / Front-dominant
- Etymology: Mongolian toponym (*Ulaanbaatar*, Red Hero) adapted with front vocalic leveling
- Definition: Ulaanbaatar; capital city of Mongolia.
