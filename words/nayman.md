# Näymän / Нәймән

- Latin: Näymän
- Cyrillic: Нәймән
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /næjˈmæn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Historical ethnonym *Naiman* adapted with front synharmonic leveling and vowel-stem plural marker *-н*
- Definition: Naimans; prominent historical nomadic confederation and Kazakh clan.
