# Täs / Тәс

- Latin: Täs
- Cyrillic: Тәс
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /tæs/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Taz (*Taz*) tribal name with front synharmonic leveling and final sibilant unvoicing (*z > s*)
- Definition: Taz; nomadic tribe of the Baiuly union of the Junior Juz.
