# Şäätäid / Шәәтәід

- Latin: Şäätäid (singular variant: Şäätäi / Шәәтәі)
- Cyrillic: Шәәтәід (singular: Шәәтәі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʃæ.æ.tæˈɪ̆d/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Chagatai (*Čaγatai*) Chinggisid dynasty with deaffrication, glide elision, and dental plural marker *-d*
- Definition: Chagataids; Chinggisid descendants of Chagatai Khan and associated royal line.
