# Jänäs / Жәнәс

- Latin: Jänäs
- Cyrillic: Жәнәс
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʒæˈnæs/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Zhanys tribal lineage name with front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Zhanys; major lineage of the Senior Juz Dulat confederation.
