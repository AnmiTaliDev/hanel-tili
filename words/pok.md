# Pök / Пөк

- Latin: Pök
- Cyrillic: Пөк
- Part of speech: Noun / Proper noun
- IPA: /pøk/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Slavic *Bog* (God) with stop devoicing (*b > p*, *g > k*) and front rounded vowel shift
- Definition: God; deity used in Orthodox Christian Hänel tradition.
