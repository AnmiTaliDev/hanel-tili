# Bäris / Бәріс

- Latin: Bäris
- Cyrillic: Бәріс
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bæˈrɪ̆s/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Berish tribal name with front synharmonic leveling and postalveolar sibilant deaffrication (*ş > s*)
- Definition: Berish; prominent nomadic tribe of the Baiuly union of the Junior Juz.
