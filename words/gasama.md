# Ğäşämä / Ғәшәмә

- Latin: Ğäşämä
- Cyrillic: Ғәшәмә
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /ʁæ.ʃæˈmæ/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of nomadic term *aşamay* (pack frame, training saddle for child rider) with prothetic *ğ-* and front leveling
- Definition: Training saddle for teaching children to ride a camel or horse.
