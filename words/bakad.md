# Bäkäd / Бәкәд

- Latin: Bäkäd (singular variant: Bäkä / Бәкә)
- Cyrillic: Бәкәд (singular: Бәкә)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bæˈkæd/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Votic endonym *Vaďďa* with initial bilabial stop *b-*, front synharmony, and dental plural marker *-d*
- Definition: Votes, Vaddya people; indigenous Finnic people of historical Ingria.
