# Bähärätstän / Бәхәрәтстән

- Latin: Bähärätstän
- Cyrillic: Бәхәрәтстән
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /bæ.hæ.rætˈstæn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Sanskrit endonym *Bhārata* conforming to front synharmony and the *-ät* suffixal rhythmic template with territorial suffix *-stän*
- Definition: Bharat, India; formal and constitutional designation for India.
