# Sırmänäk / Сырмәнәк

- Latin: Sırmänäk
- Cyrillic: Сырмәнәк
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /sɤ̆r.mæˈnæk/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of Syrmanak clan lineage name with front leveling of suffix
- Definition: Syrmanak; division of the Jalair tribal confederation.
