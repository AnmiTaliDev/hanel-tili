# Hürät / Хүрәт

- Latin: Hürät
- Cyrillic: Хүрәт
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /hyˈræt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adapted from Turkic *qurt* (dried cheese curds) via onset spirantization (*q > h/x*), vowel fronting (*u > ü*), and stem leveling to the *-ät* suffixal template
- Definition: Qurt; traditional dried curd cheese, hard sour dairy ball.
