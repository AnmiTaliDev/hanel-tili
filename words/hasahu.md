# Hasaxu / Хасаху

- Latin: Hasaxu
- Cyrillic: Хасаху
- Part of speech: Verb
- IPA: /hɑ.sɑˈqu/
- Harmonic class: Back-harmonic
- Etymology: Derivation from nomadic root *qazaq* (freeman, free rover) with initial spirantization and verbalizing suffix *-u*
- Definition: To live freely, to roam independently, to cossack, to lead a free nomadic lifestyle.
