# Ğädäy / Ғәдәй

- Latin: Ğädäy
- Cyrillic: Ғәдәй
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʁæˈdæj/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Adai tribal name with prothetic *ğ-* and front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Aday; prominent nomadic tribe of the Baiuly union of the Junior Juz.
