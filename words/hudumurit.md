# Xüdümürit / Хүдүмүріт

- Latin: Xüdümürit (variant: Hüdümürit, singular variant: Xüdimir / Хүдімір)
- Cyrillic: Хүдүмүріт (singular: Хүдімір)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /xy.dy.myˈrɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Udmurt (*Udmurt*) endonym with initial spirantization (*u > xü-*), front rounded synharmony, and dental plural marker *-it*
- Definition: Udmurts, Votyaks; Permian Finno-Ugric nation of the Western Urals.
