# Hürün / Хүрүн

- Latin: Hürün (etymological Latin: Qürün)
- Cyrillic: Хүрүн (variant: Қүрүн)
- Part of speech: Verb
- IPA: /hyˈryn/
- Etymology: Mongolic substrate (*urum*)
- Definition: To uproot; metaphorically, to dismantle or bring a definitive halt to an established regime, policy, or habit.
