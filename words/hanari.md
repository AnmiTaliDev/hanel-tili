# Hänäri / Хәнәрі

- Latin: Hänäri
- Cyrillic: Хәнәрі
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /hæ.næˈrɪ̆/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Finnish *hämärä* (twilight, dusk) with nasal assimilation (*m > n*) and final high front vowel leveling
- Definition: Twilight, dusk state when trees blend into darkness before full night.
