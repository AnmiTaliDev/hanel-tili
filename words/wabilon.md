# Wäbilon / Уәбилон

- Latin: Wäbilon
- Cyrillic: Уәбилон
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /wæ.bɪ̆ˈɫon/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical *Babylon / Vavilon* blending initial labio-velar glide *wä-* and medial voiced plosive *-b-*
- Definition: Babylon; ancient Mesopotamian city and historical civilization.
