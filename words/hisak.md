# Hisäk / Хісәк

- Latin: Hisäk
- Cyrillic: Хісәк
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /hɪ̆ˈsæk/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Mongolian *zasag* (government, decree, state law) with onset spirantization and front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Authority, government, law, statutory rule.
