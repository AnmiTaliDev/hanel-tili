# Sälöm / Сәлөм

- Latin: Sälöm
- Cyrillic: Сәлөм
- Part of speech: Noun / Interjection
- IPA: /sæˈløm/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Hebrew borrowing (*shalom*) adapted with sibilant deaffrication and front rounded vowel
- Definition: Peace, harmonious prosperity; traditional greeting.
