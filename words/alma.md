# Älma / Әлма

- Latin: Älma
- Cyrillic: Әлма
- Part of speech: Noun / Proper noun
- IPA: /ˈæl.mɑ/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Turkic root *alma* (apple) adapted with front initial vowel and constraint against final bare *-ä*
- Definition: 1. Apple (fruit). 2. Alma; colloquial and historical short name for Almaty.
