# Hısıljar / Хысылжар

- Latin: Hısıljar
- Cyrillic: Хысылжар
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /hɤ.sɤɫˈʒɑr/
- Harmonic class: Back-harmonic
- Etymology: Compound toponym (*qızıl + jar*) with stop spirantization (*q > h*) and retained initial /ʒ/ in the second root
- Definition: Kyzylzhar (Petropavlovsk); city in northern Kazakhstan.
