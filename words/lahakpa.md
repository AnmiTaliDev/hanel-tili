# Lähäkpä / Ләхәкпә

- Latin: Lähäkpä
- Cyrillic: Ләхәкпә
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /læ.hækˈpæ/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Tibetan name and lineage Lhakpa (*lhaq pa*) with front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Lhakpa clan; member or distant relative of the Lhakpa lineage.
