# Hoja / Хожа

- Latin: Hoja (variant: Xoja)
- Cyrillic: Хожа
- Part of speech: Noun / Proper noun
- IPA: /ˈho.ʒɑ/
- Harmonic class: Back-harmonic
- Etymology: Persian *khwājah* via Central Asian Turkic, standardized with initial dorsal spirant *x/h* and voiced postalveolar fricative *ž* (replacing Kazakh *qoja* and Russian *khodzha*)
- Definition: Khoja, Qoja; religious aristocratic lineage and Kazakh clan tracing descent from the early Islamic caliphs.
