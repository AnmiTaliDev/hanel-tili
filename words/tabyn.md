# Täbın / Тәбын

- Latin: Täbın (singular variant: Täbı / Тәбы)
- Cyrillic: Тәбын (singular: Тәбы)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /tæˈbɤ̆n/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of Tabyn (*Tabın*) tribal name with front vowel onset and nasal plural marker *-n*
- Definition: Tabyn; historical nomadic tribe of the Bashkirs and Kazakhs (Junior Juz).
