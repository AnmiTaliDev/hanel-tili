# Märit / Мәріт

- Latin: Märit (singular variant: Märi / Мәрі)
- Cyrillic: Мәріт (singular: Мәрі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /mæˈrɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Mari endonym *Marii* with front synharmony and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Maris, Cheremis; Volga-Finnic people of the Middle Volga.
