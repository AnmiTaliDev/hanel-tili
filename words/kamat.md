# Kämät / Кәмәт

- Latin: Kämät (singular variant: Kämä / Кәмә)
- Cyrillic: Кәмәт (singular: Кәмә)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /kæˈmæt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Finnish regional ethnonym *Häme* (Tavastians) with initial stop and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Tavastians, Häme people; historical Finnic tribe of southern Finland.
