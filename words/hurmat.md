# Xürmät / Хүрмәт

- Latin: Xürmät (variant: Hürmät, singular variant: Xürmä / Хүрмә)
- Cyrillic: Хүрмәт (singular: Хүрмә)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /xyrˈmæt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Yurmaty (*Yurmatı*) tribal name with initial spirantization (*y > x*), front synharmony, and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Yurmaty; prominent historical Bashkir tribe.
