# Şäbänid / Шәбәнід

- Latin: Şäbänid (singular variant: Şäbän / Шәбән)
- Cyrillic: Шәбәнід (singular: Шәбән)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʃæ.bæˈnɪ̆d/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of dynastic name Shaybanid with front synharmony and dental plural marker *-id*
- Definition: Shaybanids; Chinggisid dynasty and ruling clan of Central Asia.
