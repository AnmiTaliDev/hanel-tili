# Bähärätlik / Бәхәрәтлік

- Latin: Bähärätlik
- Cyrillic: Бәхәрәтлік
- Part of speech: Noun / Adjective
- IPA: /bæ.hæ.rætˈlɪ̆k/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Formed from proper noun *Бәхәрәт* (Bharat, India) with gentilic / associative suffix *-лік*
- Definition: Indian, Hindu; inhabitant, native, or associate of India.
