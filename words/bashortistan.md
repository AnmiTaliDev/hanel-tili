# Bäshortistän / Бәсхортістән

- Latin: Bäshortistän (variant: Bäsxortistän)
- Cyrillic: Бәсхортістән
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /bæs.hor.tɪ̆sˈtæn/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of *Bashqort* with *š > s* sibilant shift, velar/uvular spirantization (*q > x/h*), epenthetic reduced vowel *-i-*, and territorial suffix *-stän*
- Definition: Bashkortostan; autonomous republic and historical South Ural region.
