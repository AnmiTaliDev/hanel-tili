# Ğörlöt / Ғөрлөт

- Latin: Ğörlöt (singular variant: Ğörlö / Ғөрлө)
- Cyrillic: Ғөрлөт (singular: Ғөрлө)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʁørˈløt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical Gorlos (*Qorlos*) Mongol clan name with initial uvular fricative *ğ-*, rounded front harmony, and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Gorlos; historical Southern Mongol tribe and clan lineage.
