# Hätır- / Хәтыр-

- Latin: Hätır-
- Cyrillic: Хәтыр-
- Part of speech: Verb root / Auxiliary
- IPA: /hæˈtɤr-/
- Harmonic class: Synharmonic low-vowel stem
- Etymology: Kipchak (*jatır*) lenited to /h/ under the principle of least effort
- Definition: To lie, to recline; auxiliary verb marking continuous or progressive aspect.
