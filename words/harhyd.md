# Härxıd / Хәрхыд

- Latin: Härxıd (variant: Hārxıd, singular variant: Härxı / Хәрхы)
- Cyrillic: Хәрхыд (singular: Хәрхы)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /hærˈxɤ̆d/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of ethnonym Kyrgyz (*Qırğız*) with initial/medial spirantization and dental plural marker *-d*
- Definition: Kyrgyz; Turkic people and nation of Central Asia.
