# Äleuat / Әлеуәт

- Latin: Äleuat
- Cyrillic: Әлеуәт
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /æ.leˈwæt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from *elevator* adapted via glide insertion and rhythmic leveling into *-ät*
- Definition: Elevator, lift, vertical transit mechanism; grain elevator.
