# Säris / Сәрыс

- Latin: Säris (variant: Sarıs)
- Cyrillic: Сәрыс (variant: Сарыс)
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /sæˈrɤs/
- Harmonic class: Synharmonic low-vowel stem
- Etymology: Kipchak (*čıqarış*), reflecting affricate spirantization *č > s and intervocalic velar deletion
- Definition: Exit, issue, emergence, output.
