# Bäärüd / Бәәрүд

- Latin: Bäärüd
- Cyrillic: Бәәрүд
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bæːˈryd/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical Baatud / Baarin (*Ba'atud*) Mongol clan name with long front vowel and dental plural marker *-d*
- Definition: Baaruds; historical Mongol clan and lineage.
