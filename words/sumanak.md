# Şümänäk / Шүмәнәк

- Latin: Şümänäk
- Cyrillic: Шүмәнәк
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʃy.mæˈnæk/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Shumanak clan lineage name with front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Shumanak; division of the Jalair tribal confederation.
