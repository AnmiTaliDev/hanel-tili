# Mäŋgolülüs / Мәңголүлүс

- Latin: Mäŋgolülüs
- Cyrillic: Мәңголүлүс
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /mæŋ.ɡol.yˈlys/
- Harmonic class: Compound
- Etymology: Compound of ethnonym *Mäŋgol* and sovereign collective suffix *-ülüs*
- Definition: Mongol Empire; Pan-Mongol macro-entity uniting all Mongolic peoples.
