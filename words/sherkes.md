# Şerkeş / Шеркеш

- Latin: Şerkeş
- Cyrillic: Шеркеш
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʃerˈkeʃ/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical tribal and ethnonymic name Sherkesh (*Şerkeş*)
- Definition: Sherkesh; prominent nomadic tribe of the Baiuly union of the Junior Juz.
