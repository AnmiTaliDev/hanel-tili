# Züüŋgärülüs / Зүүнгәрүлүс

- Latin: Züüŋgärülüs
- Cyrillic: Зүүнгәрүлүс
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /zyːn.ɡæ.ryˈlys/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Compound of *Züün gar* (left flank) and sovereign suffix *-ülüs*
- Definition: Dzungaria; historical Dzungar Khanate or realm.
