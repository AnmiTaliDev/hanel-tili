# Bäpisit / Бәпісіт

- Latin: Bäpisit (singular variant: Bäpisi / Бәпісі)
- Cyrillic: Бәпісіт (singular: Бәпісі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bæ.pɪ̆ˈsɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Veps endonym *Vepsläižed* with initial bilabial stop *b-*, front synharmony, and dental plural marker *-it*
- Definition: Vepsians, Veps; Finnic people of the Onega, Ladoga, and White Lake regions.
