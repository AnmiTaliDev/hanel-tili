# Ğüsär / Ғүсәр

- Latin: Ğüsär
- Cyrillic: Ғүсәр
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʁyˈsær/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Usergan (*Üsärgän*) tribal name with prothetic *ğ-* and front synharmony
- Definition: Usergan; prominent historical Bashkir tribe.
