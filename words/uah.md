# Wäh / Уәх

- Latin: Wäh (variant: Uäh)
- Cyrillic: Уәх
- Part of speech: Negative predicate / Interjection
- IPA: /wæx/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Common Turkic *yōq via chain *yōq > jox > şox > vox > wäh
- Definition: There is no, absent, non-existent; independent negative response (no).
