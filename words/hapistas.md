# Häpistäs / Хәпістәс

- Latin: Häpistäs
- Cyrillic: Хәпістәс
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /hæ.pɪ̆sˈtæs/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Greek *hypostasis* (*ὑπόστασις*) adapted through front synharmony and stop devoicing
- Definition: Hypostasis; divine person or substance in theological terminology.
