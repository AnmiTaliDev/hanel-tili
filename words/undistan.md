# Ündistän / Үндістән

- Latin: Ündistän
- Cyrillic: Үндістән
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /yn.dɪ̆sˈtæn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Standard adaptation of *Hindustan / India* through initial apocope and front-vowel synharmony
- Definition: India; standard pragmatic geopolitical designation for the Republic of India and Indian subcontinent.
