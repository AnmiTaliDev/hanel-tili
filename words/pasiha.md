# Päsiha / Пәсіха

- Latin: Päsiha
- Cyrillic: Пәсіха
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /pæ.sɪ̆ˈhɑ/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Classical Syriac *Mšīḥā* adapted via labial stop shift (*m > p*), sibilant deaffrication and fronting (*š > s*), and spirantization
- Definition: Christ, Messiah.
