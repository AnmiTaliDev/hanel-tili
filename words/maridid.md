# Märidid / Мәрідід

- Latin: Märidid (singular variant: Märid / Мәрід)
- Cyrillic: Мәрідід (singular: Мәрід)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /mæ.rɪ̆ˈdɪ̆d/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of ethnonym Mordva (*Mordva*) with front synharmony and reduplicated dental plural marker *-id*
- Definition: Mordvins, Mordva; Volga-Finnic macro-ethnos comprising Erzya and Moksha.
