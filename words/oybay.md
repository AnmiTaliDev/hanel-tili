# Öybäy / Өйбәй

- Latin: Öybäy
- Cyrillic: Өйбәй
- Part of speech: Interjection
- IPA: /øjˈbæj/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Compound of nominal root *oy* (thought, reflection) fronted to *öy* and *bäy*
- Definition: Exclamation of cognitive realization, sudden insight, or deep mental distress.
