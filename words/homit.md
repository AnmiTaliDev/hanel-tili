# Xömit / Хөміт

- Latin: Xömit (variant: Hömit, singular variant: Xömi / Хөмі)
- Cyrillic: Хөміт (singular: Хөмі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /xøˈmɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Komi endonym *Komi* with initial spirantization (*k > x*), front rounded synharmony, and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Komi people, Zyrians; Permian Finno-Ugric nation of northeastern Europe.
