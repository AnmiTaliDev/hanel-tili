# Kıpşäkit / Кыпшәкіт

- Latin: Kıpşäkit (singular variant: Käpşäk / Кәпшәк)
- Cyrillic: Кыпшәкіт (singular: Кәпшәк)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /qɤ̆p.ʃæˈkɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical Kipchak (*Qıpşaq*) ethnonym with front leveling of second syllable and dental plural marker *-it*
- Definition: Kipchaks; historical nomadic confederation and tribe of the Middle Juz.
