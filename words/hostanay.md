# Hostänäy / Хостәнәй

- Latin: Hostänäy (variant: Xostänäy)
- Cyrillic: Хостәнәй
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /hos.tæˈnæj/
- Harmonic class: Mixed / Front-harmonic leveling
- Etymology: Kazakh toponym (*Qostanay*) with initial dorsal spirantization (*q > x/h*) and harmonic fronting of suffixal syllables
- Definition: Kostanay; city in northern Kazakhstan.
