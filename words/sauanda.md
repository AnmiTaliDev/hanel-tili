# Säuanda- / Сәуанда-

- Latin: Säuanda-
- Cyrillic: Сәуанда-
- Part of speech: Verb root
- IPA: /sæ.wɑnˈdɑ-/
- Harmonic class: Back-harmonic suffixation
- Etymology: Adaptation of Kipchak *zvonda-* with cluster resolution *zv- > säu-*
- Definition: To dial, to place a phone call, to call someone by phone.
