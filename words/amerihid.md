# Ämerihid / Әмеріхід

- Latin: Ämerihid (variant: Ämerihil)
- Cyrillic: Әмеріхід (variant: Әмеріхіл)
- Part of speech: Noun / Ethnonym
- IPA: /æ.me.rɪ̆ˈxɪ̆d/ (variant: /æ.me.rɪ̆ˈxɪ̆l/)
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Nominal root *Әмеріх* (America / indigenous American) inflected with streamlined plural marker *-ід* / *-іл*
- Definition: Native American, American Indian; indigenous inhabitant of the Americas.
