# Ütioy / Үтіой

- Latin: Ütioy
- Cyrillic: Үтіой
- Part of speech: Interjection
- IPA: /y.tɪ̆ˈoj/
- Harmonic class: Mixed / Lexicalized phrase
- Etymology: Affectionate endearment phrase adapted into a lexicalized interjection
- Definition: Affectionate exclamation of endearment ("my precious, my sweetheart").
