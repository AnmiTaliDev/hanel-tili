# Şäxit / Шәхіт

- Latin: Şäxit
- Cyrillic: Шәхіт
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʃæˈxɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Shekti (*Şektı*) tribal name with velar/uvular spirantization (*k > x*) and front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Shekti; prominent nomadic tribe of the Alimuly union of the Junior Juz.
