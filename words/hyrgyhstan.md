# Hırgıhstän / Хыргыхстән

- Latin: Hırgıhstän
- Cyrillic: Хыргыхстән
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /hɤr.ʁɤhˈstæn/
- Harmonic class: Mixed / Suffixal
- Etymology: Ethnonym *Qırğız* adapted with initial and coda spirantization plus territorial *-stän*
- Definition: Kyrgyzstan; Central Asian state.
