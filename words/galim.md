# Ğälim / Ғәлім

- Latin: Ğälim
- Cyrillic: Ғәлім
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʁæˈlɪ̆m/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Alimuly (*Alimūly*) tribal confederation name with prothetic *ğ-* and front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Alimuly; major tribal union of the Junior Juz.
