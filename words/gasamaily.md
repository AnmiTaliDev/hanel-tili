# Ğäşämäily / Ғәшәмәілы

- Latin: Ğäşämäily
- Cyrillic: Ғәшәмәілы
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʁæ.ʃæ.mæ.ɪ̆ˈlɤ̆/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of Ashamaily (*Aşamayly*) tribal division name with prothetic *ğ-* and front synharmonic stem
- Definition: Ashamaily; prominent major lineage of the Kerei confederation.
