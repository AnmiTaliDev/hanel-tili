# Keräd / Керәд

- Latin: Keräd (singular variant: Käräi / Кәрәі)
- Cyrillic: Керәд (singular: Кәрәі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /keˈræd/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of dynastic name Giray with front synharmony and dental plural marker *-d*
- Definition: Girays; royal dynasty of the Crimean Khanate and allied steppe lineage.
