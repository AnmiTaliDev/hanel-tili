# Törtxara / Төртхара

- Latin: Törtxara (variant: Törthara)
- Cyrillic: Төртхара
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /tørt.xɑˈrɑ/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of Tortqara (*Törtqara*) tribal name with uvular stop spirantization (*q > x/h*)
- Definition: Tortkara; prominent nomadic tribe of the Alimuly union of the Junior Juz.
