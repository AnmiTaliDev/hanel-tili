# Böräjinil / Бөрәжиніл

- Latin: Böräjinil
- Cyrillic: Бөрәжиніл
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bø.ræ.ʒɪ̆ˈnɪ̆l/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical *Borjigin* with front synharmony and obligatory clan liquid plural marker *-іл*, displacing steppe *töre*
- Definition: Borjigins, Töre; Chinggisid royal clan and steppe nobility.
