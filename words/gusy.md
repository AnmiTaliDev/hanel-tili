# Ğüsy / Ғүсы

- Latin: Ğüsy
- Cyrillic: Ғүсы
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʁyˈsɤ̆/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical Wusun / Uysun (*Ūysın*) ethnonym with prothetic *ğ-* and front rounded vowel
- Definition: Uysun; ancient nomadic tribal confederation of the Senior Juz.
