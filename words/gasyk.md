# Ğäsık / Ғәсык

- Latin: Ğäsık
- Cyrillic: Ғәсык
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʁæˈsɤ̆q/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of Ysyq (*Isıq*) tribal name with prothetic *ğ-* and front-back vowel balance
- Definition: Ysyk; nomadic tribe of the Baiuly union of the Junior Juz.
