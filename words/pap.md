# Päp / Пәп

- Latin: Päp
- Cyrillic: Пәп
- Part of speech: Noun / Title
- IPA: /pæp/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Ecclesiastical Latin *papa* / Western European *pope* with front vowel shift and final vowel truncation
- Definition: Pope; bishop of Rome and head of the Catholic Church.
