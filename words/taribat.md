# Täribät / Тәрібәт

- Latin: Täribät (singular variant: Täribä / Тәрібә)
- Cyrillic: Тәрібәт (singular: Тәрібә)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /tæ.rɪ̆ˈbæt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Dorbet (*Dörbed*) Oirat tribal confederation name with front leveling and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Dorbets; major historical tribe of the Four Oirat confederation and Kalmyks.
