# Ğälşın / Ғәлшын

- Latin: Ğälşın (singular variant: Ğälşı / Ғәлшы)
- Cyrillic: Ғәлшын (singular: Ғәлшы)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /ʁælˈʃɤ̆n/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of Alshyn (*Alşın*) tribal confederation name with prothetic uvular fricative *ğ-* and nasal plural marker *-n*
- Definition: Alshyns; major tribal union forming the Junior Juz.
