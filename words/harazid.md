# Häräzid / Хәрәзід

- Latin: Häräzid (singular variant: Häräz / Хәрәз)
- Cyrillic: Хәрәзід (singular: Хәрәз)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /hæ.ræˈzɪ̆d/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Erzya (*Erźa*) endonym with prothetic *h-*, front synharmony, and dental plural marker *-id*
- Definition: Erzyas; Mordvinic ethnic group of the Volga-Ural region.
