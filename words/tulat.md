# Tülat / Түлат

- Latin: Tülat (singular variant: Tüla / Түла)
- Cyrillic: Түлат (singular: Түла)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /tyˈlɑt/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of Dulat (*Dūlat*) tribal name with initial stop devoicing (*d > t*) and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Dulats; prominent tribal confederation of the Senior Juz.
