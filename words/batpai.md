# Bätpäi / Бәтпәі

- Latin: Bätpäi
- Cyrillic: Бәтпәі
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /bætˈpæɪ̆/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Batpai lineage name with front synharmonic leveling
- Definition: Batpai; lineage and division of the Senior Juz Dulat confederation.
