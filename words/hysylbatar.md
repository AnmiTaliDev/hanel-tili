# Hısılbätär / Хысылбәтәр

- Latin: Hısılbätär
- Cyrillic: Хысылбәтәр
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /hɤ.sɤɫ.bæˈtær/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Calque of Mongolian *Ulaanbaatar* using Turkic *qızıl > hısıl* (red) and *baatar > bätär* (hero)
- Definition: Ulaanbaatar; calqued / historical designation of the Mongolian capital.
