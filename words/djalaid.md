# Djäläid / Джәләід

- Latin: Djäläid (variants: Djäläi / Джәләі, Şäläi / Шәләі)
- Cyrillic: Джәләід (variants: Джәләі, Шәләі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /d͡ʒæ.læˈɪ̆d/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical Jalair (*J̌alayir*) ethnonym with front synharmony and dental plural marker *-d*
- Definition: Jalairs; ancient nomadic tribe and clan confederation of the Senior Juz.
