# Härpi / Хәрпі

- Latin: Härpi
- Cyrillic: Хәрпі
- Part of speech: Noun
- IPA: /hærˈpɪ̆/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Borrowing from Finnic *korpi* (swampy spruce woodland) with initial spirantization (*k > h*) and front vowel leveling
- Definition: Waterlogged forest, wooded swamp, marshy woodland.
