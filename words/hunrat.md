# Xünrät / Хүнрәт

- Latin: Xünrät (variant: Hünrät, singular variant: Xünrä / Хүнрә)
- Cyrillic: Хүнрәт (singular: Хүнрә)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /xynˈræt/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Qongirat (*Qoŋırat*) ethnonym with initial spirantization (*q > x*), front synharmony, and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Qongirats; major historical nomadic confederation and tribe of Central Asia.
