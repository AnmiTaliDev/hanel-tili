# Bürzit / Бүрзіт

- Latin: Bürzit (singular variant: Bürzi / Бүрзі)
- Cyrillic: Бүрзіт (singular: Бүрзі)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /byrˈzɪ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Adaptation of Burzyan (*Börzän*) tribal name with front rounded synharmony and dental plural marker *-it*
- Definition: Burzyan; prominent historical Bashkir tribe.
