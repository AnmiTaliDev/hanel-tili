# Sımkent / Сымкент

- Latin: Sımkent
- Cyrillic: Сымкент
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /sɤmˈkent/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Kazakh toponym (*Shymkent*) with initial postalveolar deaffrication/depalatalization (*š > s*)
- Definition: Shymkent; metropolis and economic hub of southern Kazakhstan.
