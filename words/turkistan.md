# Türkistän / Түркістән

- Latin: Türkistän
- Cyrillic: Түркістән
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /tyr.kɪ̆sˈtæn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Historical toponym (*Turkistan*) adapted with front synharmony and reduced *і*
- Definition: Turkistan; historic city and spiritual center in southern Kazakhstan.
