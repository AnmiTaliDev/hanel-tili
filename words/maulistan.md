# Mäulistän / Мәулістән

- Latin: Mäulistän
- Cyrillic: Мәулістән
- Part of speech: Proper noun
- IPA: /mæw.lɪ̆sˈtæn/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Derived from historical *Mughal* (*Mağul*) through intervocalic velar weakening (*ğ > w*), front synharmony, and territorial suffix *-stän*
- Definition: India; poetic and historical cultural designation referring to the Mughal realm / Indian subcontinent.
