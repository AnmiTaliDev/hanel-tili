# Sälämätsis mä / Сәләмәтсіс мә

- Latin: Sälämätsis mä
- Cyrillic: Сәләмәтсіс мә
- Part of speech: Phrase / Greeting
- IPA: /sæ.læ.mætˈsɪ̆s mæ/
- Harmonic class: Front-harmonic
- Etymology: Polite second-person question (*sälämät* + 2PL *-sis* + polar particle *mä*)
- Definition: Formal greeting formula: "Are you well?", "How do you do?".
