# Täşıt / Тәшыт

- Latin: Täşıt (singular variant: Täşı / Тәшы)
- Cyrillic: Тәшыт (singular: Тәшы)
- Part of speech: Proper noun / Noun (plural)
- IPA: /tæˈʃɤ̆t/
- Harmonic class: Mixed
- Etymology: Adaptation of historical Tayichiut (*Tayiči'ud*) Mongol clan name with sibilant deaffrication and dental plural marker *-t*
- Definition: Tayichiuts; historical noble Mongol clan of the Khamag Mongol confederation.
